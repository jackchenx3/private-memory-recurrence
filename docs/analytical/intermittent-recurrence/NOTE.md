# Intermittent recurrence with unchanged adjacent-target statistics

Prospective mathematical context, 22 September 2026. This is a candidate next direction after ORG-DIRECTION-048, not an execution assignment or a population result.

The accepted population evidence contrasts independent targets with exact alternation of two targets. Exact alternation is a restrictive form of historical predictability. A useful separate question is whether the rare-memory advantage survives intermittent recurrence while the marginal one-step environmental changes remain fixed.

Let T0 and T1 be independent uniform n-bit targets. For each t≥2, independently copy T(t−2) with probability q; otherwise draw a fresh independent uniform target. The q=0 and q=1 cases recover IID renewal and exact two-target alternation. A single q=1/2 challenge is a symmetric mixture of these mechanisms; it is a specified interpolation, not an estimated biological frequency or a coefficient chosen to obtain a favorable outcome.

The even and odd subsequences are independent Markov chains with uniform stationary marginals. Therefore every adjacent pair remains independent uniform for every q, and its Hamming distance has the same Binomial(n,1/2) distribution. Two-step dependence changes:

- P(Tt=y | T(t−2)=x) = q 1[y=x] + (1−q)/2^n.
- The probability of an exact two-step return is q + (1−q)/2^n; accidental equality after refresh is retained.
- Expected two-step Hamming distance is n(1−q)/2.
- The one-bit sign correlation at lag two is q; at each even lag 2k it is q^k. Odd-lag pairs are independent. Whole-target k-step parity-chain transitions replace q by q^k.

These are unconditional target-law properties, not independence of targets from an adapted population or its preparation. A resident population prepared on the first pair carries information about those targets. Using identical prepared states and the same first pair under every q can hold that preparation fixed while changing subsequent recurrence. The first two target presentations then match exactly across conditions; later paths diverge. It does not turn the q=0 population assay into an independent, neutral-start training replication.

A prospective bounded comparison could retain the 192 prepared recurrent resident states and unchanged six private-memory/fresh/sham policies from 047. Keep the first pair and all local, scout, fresh, founder and tie streams fixed. Authenticate the 1,152 exact-recurrence paths as stored controls. Generate one new set of target innovations and fair copy/refresh decisions per block to compare q=0 and q=1/2, requiring 2,304 new paths in total. Both new laws would use the same innovation candidates. The q=1/2 recursion must copy its own target from two steps earlier, not merely pick from the original two-target pair. No state, continuation or target is filtered by survival or success.

The scientifically distinct endpoint is historical-minus-fresh carrier change under intermittent recurrence. Absolute mean increase and historical-minus-sham attribution are separate requirements for retrieval-attributed spread. Direct recurrence-law contrasts assess attenuation without comparing separate significance labels. Both raw population accuracy and all costly failures remain. A positive primary would establish a limited robustness result; a negative or unresolved result would delimit the exact-recurrence finding. No monotone response, universal crossover or local-to-population prediction is assumed.

The accompanying exact rational check enumerates all 4,096 six-time, two-bit target sequences for q=0,1/2,1, verifying normalization, every adjacent-pair probability, every lag-two probability and its expected distance. It generates no pseudorandom values or population trajectories. The general identities follow from the independent parity-chain construction.
